## About me

I'm a Master's student in **Information Systems at the University of Agder (UiA)** in Kristiansand, Norway. My main interest is **digital transformation**: how organisations can use technology thoughtfully to improve processes, services and collaboration.

I'm interested in the link between people, organisational needs and technology. My experience spans process mapping and digitalisation in the public sector, requirements gathering and software development at Phonero, and student representation focused on responsible digitalisation. I enjoy understanding a problem before choosing or building a solution, and thinking about how that solution will work for the people who use it.

**Areas I'm interested in:**

`Digital transformation` `Process improvement` `Requirements analysis` `System development` `Responsible digitalisation` `Digital sovereignty` `AI in education and work`

## Selected work

### [Personal portfolio](https://felixknutsen.com) | [Source code](https://github.com/FelixKnutsen/portfolio)

A bilingual portfolio built with **React, TypeScript and Vite**, designed around an interactive process-map concept.

- Norwegian and English content with language-aware routing.
- A consistent visual system linking projects, experience and case studies.
- Accessibility considerations, including support for reduced motion.
- Type checking, linting, automated tests and production builds.

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Vitest` `ESLint` `Vercel`

### [STA website project](https://github.com/FelixKnutsen/stastudentphp) | [Preview](https://stastudentphp.vercel.app/)

Website tooling for **Studentorganisasjonen i Agder (STA)**, including WordPress theme and plugin code, editor documentation and a local preview workflow. The project combines development with the practical needs of the people maintaining a public-facing website.

`PHP` `WordPress` `JavaScript` `HTML` `CSS`

### Receipt storage app | Planned personal project

A personal project idea focused on making digital receipts easier to organise and retrieve. The aim is to explore a simple, maintainable way to keep purchase documents in one place and make them easier to find when needed.

**Current stage:** Defining the scope and core requirements. Features and technology choices are still to be decided.

`Personal project` `Requirements exploration` `Application design`

## Experience at the intersection of technology and organisations

- **Digitalisation and process improvement | Kristiansand Municipality (Jan–May 2026)**  
  As an Innovation Scout, mapped work processes in the Planning and Building Department and identified opportunities for digitalisation and efficiency. Explored new technologies and communicated solution ideas based on the municipality's needs.

- **System development and requirements | Phonero (Aug 2024–Jun 2025)**  
  During a developer internship and bachelor's project, worked with production code, bug fixes, new functionality and testing. Developed a digital equipment register for business customers, using user interviews, requirements analysis, prototyping and iterative development in collaboration with the development team.

- **Responsible digitalisation and student representation | STA and NITO (2026–present)**  
  In student leadership roles, work with representation and organisational development, with involvement in digital sovereignty, data security, responsible digitalisation, digital campus and guidance on AI in education and working life.

- **Leadership and collaboration | NITO Studentene Kristiansand (2025–2026)**  
  Chaired the local student board, organising activities and strengthening collaboration between students, the professional community and NITO.
